Corner 3-Point Series
Shooting3-PointIntermediatePartner
Catch & shoot from both corners and both wings with a partner feeding the ball. Shooter rotates through 5 spots — 5 attempts per spot per round. Focus on footwork on the catch, quick release, and consistent follow-through.
15 min 3 rounds × 5 spots Basketball HoopIQ Library
Coaching cues
• Set your feet before the ball arrives — "ready stance" on every catch
• Hold the follow-through until the ball hits the rim
• Keep your shooting shoulder aligned to the basket
• Communicate with your partner before each rotation
